## July 24, 2026 — Dog Days Begin — Heliacal Rising of Sirius, July 23. Around this date in mid-northern latitudes, Sirius (α Canis Majoris, the Dog Star, the brightest star in Earth's night sky) reappears in the pre-dawn sky after weeks lost in the Sun's glare.
