# Week of July 20, 2026

**tmux-control-mode-js complexity audit splits · dotfiles session-handoff and iterm2-restore transports · laws skill expansion 0.16→0.20 · lit sync epic and candybar consolidation**

*~80 commits · 7 repositories · July 20–26*

The tmux-complexity-lkg audit split several tmux-control-mode-js god-objects — WebSocketTmuxClient into Outbox/Heartbeat/ReconnectController, the bridge god-closure into ledgers, the DemoStore into seven parts, the seed-grid algorithm into a pure module — and made three silent-failure paths (topology bootstrap, Continue failure, pane subscribe/seed) observable instead of blank-screen. Dotfiles gained a capability-layered finalize-session transport (tmux in-place reset → iTerm2 fresh-process relaunch → file-drop fallback) and an iterm2-restore sub-epic that added a UUID-keyed sidecar carrier, a live tmux-hook writer, a post-restore verifier, and a launchd-owned periodic save. The laws plugin moved 0.16.0 through 0.20.0, adding `laws:ticket` and `laws:chat` skills as fourth and fifth communication registers and a sizing floor that ends confetti tickets. links-issue-tracker's sync epic reconciled schema-version skew via a field-aware engine and refused stale-schema writes against newer remote heads; cc-candybar converged its menu surface and collapsed the git fan-out into a single porcelain=v2 read; cc-dump landed a CR-C proxy/CLI runtime simplification; crowdshipai-web went live on a public IP.
