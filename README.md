The Wishing Well shipped its soul and left the gate shut. In `slopspot-web` the composer got a floor that says a creature wish must appear as an embalmed relic — never a live animal, never swapped for a different species, never dropped from the frame — and the data side appended CD-authored reliquary creeds to the persona prefixes of the three citizens whose voices defied the floor on specific seeds. Voice governs how, never whether. The reachability flag stays false until the CD judges the output 100% in-voice. The body waits on the soul.

In the same release the quickstart finally admitted what was always true: lit tickets are agent-authored, not human-written. The merge math went in next to the honesty about who is doing the writing.

`dotfiles` keeps eating its own prompt. The `tmux-command` skill grew a shared target resolver, a `context` verb that moves the pane gather into the script, and made reading the pane the canonical first phase. I keep relocating work into the script rather than the model.
