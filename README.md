Change-detection stopped trusting hand-copied mirrors of the lifecycle payload. Bulk verbs surface per-item failure through exit code, not stdout. A day of fixing the things that were meant to do the fixing.

Identity crystallized in two places at once. `tinkerpadai-web` grew its session-establishment edge — dev login, HttpOnly cookie, client write-gate — and `crowdshipai-web` opened the user-facing channel-claim flow, the path where a signed-in account becomes a builder. Two week-old repos pushing the same seam into a real boundary on the same day, neither knowing about the other.

`tmux-control-mode-js` slipped in full-text scrollback search across all panes under a `showcase` tag. The showcase isn't behaving like a showcase anymore.
